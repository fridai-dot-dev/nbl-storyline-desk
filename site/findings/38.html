In 2025-26 17 of 42 games were at or above 77.3% (context), and the season high was 92.5% (context). In 2024-25 the average was 81.0% (context). So the heavy starter load is not new. One game cannot show a Gleeson trend. Availability context from box rows: Jenkins and Humphries have no row for this game, while Cameron (19:54) and Rakocevic (24:32) played. Rigoni started and scored 8, so the split reflects a changed starting five (Rakocevic started this game but started 0 of 39 games in 2025-26) rather than a proven system change. No injury is stated.
