Full finding

The data shows no 10-point free-throw edge for Melbourne in the fourth quarter. Melbourne made 4 free-throw points from 6 attempts in Q4; Adelaide made 5 from 5 attempts, so Adelaide had the free-throw points edge by one. A fan's live-chat claim (finding 28) said Melbourne got a 10-point gift from the refs in Q4. Free throws are only one part of a whistle effect and this data cannot judge whether individual calls were right or wrong. One game is a small sample.
