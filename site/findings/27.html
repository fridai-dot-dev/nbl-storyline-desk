Storyline Desk · Finding 27

Pre-game frame: Anthony as MVP candidate against the Cotton barometer

Before the Round 1 opener, NBL.com.au framed Melbourne v Adelaide as a test of whether Cole Anthony is an MVP candidate against Bryce Cotton, whom Chris Anstey called the barometer. Anstey also expected a big Cotton game. Anthony came in after a calf complaint that kept him out of the Blitz and limited his pre-season, and said he would be ready. This is the pre-game frame the game tested. It makes no claim about in-game numbers.
