  • Adelaide's 77% starter scoring share sits near last season's norm · Adelaide's starters scored 75 of 97 points (77.3%) against Melbourne, only modestly above their 2025-26 average starter share of 74.9% under Mike Wells (42 games). In 2025-26 17 of 42 games were at or above 77.3% (context), and the season high was 92.5% (context). In 2024-25 the average was 81.0% (context). So the heavy starter load is not new. One game cannot show a Gleeson trend. Availability context from box rows: Jenkins and Humphries have no row for this game, while Cameron (19:54) and Rakocevic (24:32) played. Rigoni started and scored 8, so the split reflects a changed starting five (Rakocevic started this game but started 0 of 39 games in 2025-26) rather than a proven system change. No injury is stated. Starter flags were clean for Adelaide (five starters in every game), with one 2016-17 data gap in the points totals.
  • Winning despite a 30-point bench deficit has happened 18 times since 2015-16 · User follow-up to finding 34. In 18 of 74 valid games since at least 2015-16 where one side's bench out-scored the other's by 30 or more, the team with the weaker bench still won, including Adelaide's 97-95 win over Melbourne (bench gap 30). Context: the bench-winning side won the other 56 of those 74 (about 76 percent). Adelaide's 30-point deficit is not the record: the largest overcome is 39 (Illawarra 102-100 in match 2636766, Sydney 106-97 in 2116390, Perth 95-86 in 579823). Data exclusions: 63 of 3,160 team-games (about 2 percent) dropped because starter flags did not total five, starter or points values were null (2017-18 has 10 null-flag team-games), or player points did not sum to the box_team score. Games where either side was dropped are excluded. Small sample: 74 games, so the rate is indicative only.
