Full finding

Melbourne took 3 field-goal attempts in the final 30 seconds of Q4, all misses (score stayed 95-97): Cole Anthony a fade-away 2 (0:17, Luke Travers offensive rebound), Chris Goulding a 3 (0:12, Joe Ingles offensive rebound), then Fabijan Krslovic a 3 (0:03), after which Adelaide took the defensive rebound. Anthony took 1 of the 3 and did not take the last shot, but he did shoot first in the sequence, so the data partly supports "Anthony didn't get the last look" (he did not take the final shot) and challenges any suggestion that he was frozen out (he took the first attempt, and his 0:56 three cut the gap to 95-97). Context: before that, Anthony's 3 at 0:56 made it 95-97.
