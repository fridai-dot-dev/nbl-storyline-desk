Full finding

In 2025-26 Chris Goulding made 2.3 threes per game, 69 from 225 attempts (7.5 per game, 30.7%) over 30 games. That is the baseline for the Anthony-creates-easier-looks storyline. It is one season, so a single game will be noisy. CONFIRMS the storyline if Goulding makes 4 or more threes, or shoots clearly above 30.7% on at least 7 attempts. CONTRADICTS it if he makes 2 or fewer or his volume falls well below 7.5 attempts, which would suggest no easier looks.
