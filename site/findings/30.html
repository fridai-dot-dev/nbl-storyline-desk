Full finding

Adelaide scored 18 more points from three-pointers than Melbourne (39 from 13 of 30, against 21 from 7 of 23) in a 97-95 win. Melbourne was ahead on two-pointers (29 of 48 against 19 of 36), which is why the game stayed close. Context: the live commentary call of 13 of 27 came at 11:21, and the final was 13 of 30. One game is a small sample, and three-point shooting regresses heavily.
