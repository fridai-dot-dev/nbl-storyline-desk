Full finding

Bryce Cotton averages 21.21 points in 39 games against Melbourne United in the dataset since 2015-16 (high 36), below his 25.3 ppg across 42 games for Adelaide in 2025-26. In his three games against Melbourne as an Adelaide player he scored 33, 22 and 17. Small sample, and the 21.21 mostly reflects Perth years. CONFIRMS the benchmark if Cotton scores around 21 or fewer, which would point to Melbourne's defence holding him down. CONTRADICTS it if he reaches 25 or more, in line with his season form, which would suggest the Melbourne history matters little.
